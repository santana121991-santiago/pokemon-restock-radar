IPHONE SETUP — SAM'S POKEMON RESTOCK RADAR

1. In ChatGPT, download pokemon-restock-radar.zip.
2. Open the iPhone Files app, go to Downloads, and tap the ZIP once. A normal folder will appear.
3. In Safari, open:
   https://github.com/santana121991-santiago/pokemon-restock-radar
4. Tap "Add file" and then "Upload files."
5. Tap "Choose your files" > "Browse," open the unzipped folder, select all seven files, and upload them.
6. Scroll down and tap "Commit changes."
7. In the repository, open restock-alerts.yml and tap the pencil/Edit button.
8. Change its filename to:
   .github/workflows/restock-alerts.yml
9. Tap "Commit changes" again.
10. Return to ChatGPT. The next step will privately connect ntfy and run the first test.

Do not put your ntfy topic inside any public file. It will be added later as a protected GitHub Actions secret.
